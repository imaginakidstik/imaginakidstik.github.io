Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Imagina Kids Poster ("the App"), an internal tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account @imaginakids0, handles information.

1. Information we access

When the account holder authorizes the App through TikTok Login, the App receives an access token and basic profile information (such as display name and open ID) needed to publish videos to that account. The App uses only the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish.

2. How we use it

This information is used exclusively to upload, publish and schedule videos on the operator's own TikTok account. We do not sell, rent or share it with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Children's privacy

The App does not collect any information from viewers of the channel, including children. It only interacts with the operator's own account.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely and are used only by the operator. They can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app settings, after which the App can no longer access the account.

5. Data deletion

The operator may request deletion of any stored data at any time; revoking the App's access in TikTok also stops all further access.

6. Changes

We may update this Policy. The updated version will be posted on this page.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator through the TikTok account @imaginakids0.
